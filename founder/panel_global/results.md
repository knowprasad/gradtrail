## What would flip a no

- A short no-credential demo that proves it runs on an ordinary laptop and shows relevant live listings from my countries.
- A short no-key demo with recently checked listings, visible original links and deadlines, plus a setup path that works on a normal laptop in under 15 minutes.
- A clear local-only setup guide with a no-paid-API demo and a simple way to back up my data.
- A safe, no-key demo that shows the workflow and a setup guide with real time and cost expectations; ideally it works on my laptop without maintaining a server.
- A clearly documented local setup that works without AI or paid keys, plus a short demo showing exactly where my resume and application data stays.
- A short privacy and data-flow explanation plus a demo I can use with sample data before configuring any accounts.
- A no-key demo with a transparent list of supported countries and sources, including the original referral links I would use.
- A simple hosted trial with clear source-by-source freshness and deadlines, so I can see it saves time before setting anything up.
- A short, no-credential demo on my laptop that shows the source coverage and lets me track one real application.
- A simple install I can finish in a few minutes, with a demo search and a clear estimate of any provider costs.
- A short demo showing a project-based resume draft with no invented details, plus a clear setup and cost estimate.
- A no-setup demo with a few current roles and clear original links, followed by a setup guide that works on a modest laptop.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
