## What would flip a no

- A free demo showing current Indian campus and off-campus technical internships from sources I already use, with clear source coverage.
- A live demo showing recent, paid internship listings with links to the original posts and clear eligibility details.
- A simple mobile-friendly demo showing supported India postings and application tracking without a complicated setup.
- A live demo showing verified, current internship listings with original-post links and clear paid/unpaid details.
- A quick phone demo showing me adding an opportunity and tracking its assessment and follow-ups in a few taps.
- A no-setup trial showing current, verified internship and campus-drive listings with clear eligibility, stipend, city and duration filters.
- A quick demo showing relevant, fresh listings I couldn't find on my usual sites, with setup handled for me.
- A quick demo showing relevant India campus roles from sources I already use, with no hosting setup on my side.
- A simple hosted version with clear setup help and a free trial using real Indian campus-role sources.
- A hosted version with useful, verified internship listings and a quick way to organize applications.
- A clear demo showing reliable campus and internship listings alongside application tracking would make me try it.
- A free, no-setup version that shows strong India internship listings I can’t easily find myself.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
