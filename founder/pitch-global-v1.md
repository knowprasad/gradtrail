GradTrail is a free, open-source, self-hosted job-search and application workspace for students, recent graduates, and early-career developers.
Search supported job sources, filter by role, location, work mode, experience or opportunity type, and posting date, then open the original listing.
Save opportunities and organize application stages, deadlines, and follow-ups. Build application materials from your profile and projects; configured AI can draft text for you to review.
You run GradTrail on your own machine or server. Some job sources and AI features require provider accounts or API keys, and your hosting or API usage may have costs. Coverage varies by source; no hosted plan or commercial price is currently offered.
