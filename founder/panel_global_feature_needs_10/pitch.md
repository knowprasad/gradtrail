GradTrail is a free, open-source app you can run on your own computer to search supported job sources and track applications.
It filters openings by role, location, work mode, experience, opportunity type, and posting date, and links back to the original post.
It can help organize deadlines and follow-ups, tailor application materials from your profile, and prepare interview practice prompts.
You control where your profile and application data are stored. AI drafting is optional and needs a configured provider; some job sources need keys, while YC and Hacker News discovery does not.
Getting started currently requires Docker and following the setup guide; this is a self-hosted app, not a hosted one-click service.
