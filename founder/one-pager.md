# Northstar · One-page business brief

## The problem

Students find internships and campus-placement opportunities across job boards, placement-cell messages, social posts, and referrals. Requirements, deadlines, compensation, and application status can be scattered. This problem statement is a hypothesis awaiting customer interviews.

## The customer

Indian college students, roughly 18–24, looking for technical internships or campus placements, especially those using coursework and projects to demonstrate experience.

## The offer and price assumption

A self-hosted workspace to find opportunities, inspect their original source, prepare a project-grounded resume and cover letter, and track deadlines and follow-ups. The current pitch assumes the software is free; hosting, AI, and search-provider costs are not included. No commercial price has been set.

## The evidence so far

- **Competitors:** Internshala and Unstop already serve students; LinkedIn, Naukri, and specialist platforms cover broad or tech-focused discovery. Many basic filters and workflow tools already exist.
- **Possible gap:** make student eligibility, stipend/duration, source freshness, deadlines, project evidence, and follow-up clearer in one workflow. This is not validated yet.
- **Data constraint:** TechReferrals has relevant referral posts, but its terms prohibit bulk copying and automated scraping. Treat it as a link-out or pursue permission for a feed.
- **Simulated panel:** 0 of 20 buyers chose the current offer. Seventeen passed mainly over self-hosting and provider-key setup. The repeated change condition was a hosted, phone-friendly demo with current India roles, clear eligibility, and original links. This is qualitative simulated feedback, not real customer evidence or a conversion forecast.
- **Customer evidence:** no real interviews or survey results yet. The panel's income values are approximate USD equivalents of low INR student-income assumptions because the panel tool only formats USD.

## Main risk and next step

The target is students, but the current product is self-hosted and needs server/database setup and provider credentials. Interview five students this week about how they find roles, verify eligibility, track applications, and whether a hosted, phone-friendly workflow would solve a meaningful problem. Show a no-setup demo and ask them to complete a real search-and-save task.

## Ask

Validate the setup burden and opportunity-data needs with five students before investing in more development. The plan is incomplete until pricing, unit economics, and real customer validation exist.
